# SipChill — Smart Active Cooling Tumbler

## What is SipChill?

SipChill is a portable, rechargeable smart beverage bottle with an active electronic cooling engine. Unlike standard insulated flasks that only slow down warming, SipChill actively pulls heat out of room-temperature drinks to chill them on demand and maintain precise target temperatures.

---

## Key Highlights

* **Active Chilling On-Demand:** Uses a solid-state electronic cooling element (Peltier module) instead of bulky, noisy compressors.
* **Thermal Heat Buffer:** Employs a specialized phase change wax around the cooling element to silently absorb heat spikes during rapid cooling.
* **Even Cooling Without Moving Parts:** Uses silent ultrasonic micro-vibrations through the base to circulate fluid, preventing cold spots and thermal stratification without stirrers or blades.
* **Modular Two-Piece Design:**
  * **Chilling Base:** Contains the rechargeable battery pack, cooling element, and USB-C fast-charging electronics.
  * **Beverage Vessel:** Completely detaches from the base, making it food-grade, waterproof, and easy to clean like a standard tumbler.
* **Smart App & Touch Controls:** Connects via Bluetooth and features a touch-enabled cap, allowing users to select an exact temperature setpoint that the system maintains automatically.

---

## How It Works

1. **Pour & Set:** Add any beverage and choose your desired temperature using the cap display or smartphone app.
2. **Rapid Cool:** The base module activates solid-state heat pumping while silent micro-vibrations circulate the liquid for even cooling.
3. **Thermal Absorption:** The internal heat buffer absorbs waste heat to keep the outer chassis comfortable and quiet.
4. **Maintain:** Once the target temperature is reached, the system throttles power to hold the temperature efficiently on battery power.
